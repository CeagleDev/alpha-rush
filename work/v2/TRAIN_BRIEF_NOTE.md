# The sixth location: BULLET TRAIN (zone id `train`, revealed from 1,230 m)

Concept for the Atlas reference (to be generated once Atlas is reconnected, opened for the owner, and approved
before any geometry): "Environment concept art for an endless runner: running along the ROOF of a white
bullet train at night as it streaks through a Tokyo-like city, seen from behind at running height; the roof is a
flat white/silver spine with ribbed panels, cable ducts and low pantograph frames; carriage joints every 25 m;
the train is on an elevated viaduct so the city lights, billboards and towers rush past below and beside; blue
light-streaks and sparks; the sky deep blue; no people, no text; wide straight roof with room for three lanes,
perspective centred."

Zone plan (mirrors rooftops): height 6 (deck), variants N0..N5, lightsMood 'neon' (cool blue), obstacles:
jump `roof_vent_box` (1.0 m), roll `pantograph_frame` (bar at 1.3 m, legs outside the lane), block
`carriage_end_bulkhead` (2 m, one lane, with a lit door). Side dressing: viaduct parapet strips, passing
billboard towers set back, catenary masts every 15 m, light-streak sprites (emissive strips angled along z),
the far city via farband. Speed feel: the zone's cable ducts and sleepers give a strong forward texture.
